FAQ

Is Germany still a good business location in 2026?

It depends on the business. For high-volume, low-margin, cost-sensitive production, the burdens are heavy. For quality-driven, knowledge-based or relationship-based businesses, Germany's stability, skilled workforce and quality reputation remain real advantages. The honest answer isn't 'good' or 'bad' — it's 'good for whom, and used how'.

How do SMEs deal with German bureaucracy?

By treating it as a manageable process rather than a personal grievance. The businesses that suffer most are those that handle it reactively and inconsistently. Those that build efficient routines — or delegate them — turn a shared burden into a relative advantage, because the same bureaucracy blocks their competitors and foreign entrants too.

What are Germany's advantages for SMEs?

A large, stable, loyal domestic market; an exceptional vocational training system despite the labor shortage; strong legal certainty and infrastructure; and the global weight of a quality reputation. These are precisely the advantages that don't make headlines — which is why the businesses that use them deliberately gain an edge.

Should I relocate my business abroad?

Rarely the right first answer. Relocation solves some cost problems while creating new ones — distance from your market, loss of the quality reputation, cultural and legal complexity. Before considering it, most SMEs have unused levers at home: pricing, focus, efficiency. Relocation is a major strategic decision, not a shortcut around domestic challenges.
