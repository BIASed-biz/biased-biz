FAQ

What does a beauty salon website need most?

Two things above all: beautiful, high-quality photos that show your work, and effortless online booking. Clients decide on the visual impression and book only if it's easy. Everything else — services, prices, location — supports these two. A beautiful site that makes booking hard fails; an easy-to-book site with weak photos underdelivers.

Do I need online booking?

For most beauty businesses, yes — it's increasingly expected and removes the friction of phone-tag. Clients often browse and book outside business hours; without online booking, you lose those. Even a simple booking link dramatically outperforms 'call to book' for conversion.

How do I get more clients from my website?

Show your work in strong photos, make booking effortless, display genuine reviews, and ensure you're found locally via Google. Most beauty-business websites lose clients at the booking step — either it's too hard or absent. Fixing that single step often does more than any redesign.
