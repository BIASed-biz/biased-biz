FAQ

How do I build a sales system that runs without me?

In four steps: define exactly who the target customer is, create a repeatable process for reaching them, structure the follow-up so no lead is lost, and document it so someone other than you can run it. The goal is a process that produces predictable results from predictable inputs — not one that depends on your presence and memory.

Why do referrals alone not scale?

Referrals are valuable but unpredictable — you can't control when they come or how many. A business relying only on referrals has no lever to grow deliberately. A sales system adds a controllable channel on top of referrals, so growth becomes a decision rather than a hope.

How long until a sales system shows results?

A clear target-customer definition and structured follow-up can improve close rates within weeks. Building a full system — approach, pipeline, onboarding — typically takes 30–90 days. The QuickWin workshop identifies the single biggest sales leak in half a day.

Do I need a CRM to build a sales system?

A CRM helps but isn't the starting point. The system is the thinking — who you target, how you reach them, how you follow up. A simple structured spreadsheet can run a sales system at the start. The tool matters far less than whether the process is clear and actually followed.
