FAQ

What does a tradesperson's website need?

Three things above all: photos of your completed work (the strongest trust signal), obvious trust markers (reviews, certifications, service area), and effortless contact (visible phone number, one-tap call on mobile). Homeowners searching for a tradesperson are cautious and decide fast — proof of quality and easy contact turn a searcher into an inquiry.

Do I need a website if I get work by referral?

Even referral-driven trades benefit: when someone refers you, the first thing the new customer does is look you up. A professional website confirms the referral and closes the trust gap. Without one, a referred customer may hesitate or check a competitor who does have a credible online presence.

How do I get more inquiries from my website?

Show real photos of completed work, display genuine reviews and certifications, state clearly what you do and where, and make contact effortless — a visible phone number and one-tap call on mobile. Most trades websites lose inquiries by hiding the phone number or failing to prove reliability. Fix those and inquiries rise.
