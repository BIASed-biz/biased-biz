FAQ

What does a restaurant website need?

Above all: a current menu that loads instantly on mobile, obvious opening hours and location, and an easy way to reserve or order. Guests decide fast and on their phones. A slow site, a hidden or outdated menu, or unclear hours sends them straight to a competitor. Get these fundamentals right before anything fancy.

Should the menu be a PDF?

Ideally not. PDFs load slowly on mobile, are hard to read on a small screen, and many guests won't open them. A menu built directly into the page loads instantly and reads easily on any phone — which is where nearly all restaurant searches happen. The easier the menu is to see, the more guests you convert.

Do I need online ordering or reservations?

It depends on your model, but making it easy for guests to take the next step — reserve a table or place an order — significantly improves conversion. Even if you rely on walk-ins, a simple reservation option captures guests who plan ahead. The key is removing friction from whatever action you want guests to take.
