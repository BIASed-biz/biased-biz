01

When a website is essential

If customers research before buying, if you need to explain something complex, if you compete on trust and professionalism, if you want to be found for what you offer — then yes, a website earns its place.

02

When a Google profile may be enough

For some very local, walk-in or referral-driven businesses, a complete Google Business Profile carries most of what a simple website would — at least to start.

03

What a website must actually do

A website that just 'exists' is decoration. A working website does one job: turn the right visitor into an inquiry. Clear, fast, trustworthy, with an obvious next step.

04

What a website should not be

Not a brochure nobody reads, not a vanity project, not a 40-page maze. The most common website mistake is building for the owner's pride instead of the customer's decision.

FAQ

Does every business really need a website?

No — despite what agencies say. It depends on whether your customers research online before buying, whether you need to explain or build trust, and whether being found matters for your model. For many businesses a website is essential; for some very local or referral-driven ones, a strong Google Business Profile carries most of the weight to start. The honest question is what the website would actually do for you.

What should a small business website cost?

It varies widely, but beware two extremes: the cheap template that looks like everyone else's and converts nobody, and the expensive custom project that overshoots what an SME needs. BI³AS³ed builds SME websites at a fixed price from €1,490, live in 30 days — enough to do the job well without paying for complexity you don't need. Fixed price means no open-ended cost.

What makes a website actually work?

One job, done well: turning the right visitor into an inquiry. That means it's fast, clear about what you offer and for whom, trustworthy in appearance, and has an obvious next step on every page. Most websites fail not from bad design but from no clear purpose — they exist rather than convert. Clarity beats cleverness.

How long does it take to build a website?

A focused SME website can be live in about 30 days — long enough to do it properly, short enough to avoid the endless projects that drag for months. The delay is usually not the building but the deciding: what it's for, what it says, what the customer should do. Clarity upfront makes the build fast.
