What makes a website 'premium'?

Not decoration — effectiveness combined with a professional look. A premium website builds trust at first glance, is clear about what you offer and for whom, loads fast, works perfectly on mobile, and guides the visitor toward an inquiry. Premium means it does its job well and looks the part — not that it's expensive or elaborate for its own sake.

How long until my website is live?

30 days. Long enough to do it properly — clear positioning, real content, conversion-focused structure — and short enough to avoid the endless web projects that drag for months and cost far more. The fixed timeline is part of the discipline: it forces the decisions that make a website work.

Why fixed price instead of hourly?

Because hourly billing gives you an open-ended cost and no certainty. A fixed price means you know exactly what you'll pay before you start, and the incentive is aligned — the goal is a working website, not billable hours. For an SME, cost certainty is as valuable as the website itself.
