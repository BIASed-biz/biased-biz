The nearest levers

01

Pricing

The fastest lever. A well-communicated price increase flows almost directly to revenue and profit. Most SMEs underprice out of fear — and leave the easiest revenue on the table.

02

Existing customers

Selling more to those who already trust you is far cheaper than winning new ones. Upsell, cross-sell, higher frequency — the warm revenue most businesses underuse.

You may not need more leads — you may need to close more of the ones you have. A small improvement in close rate can outweigh a large increase in lead volume.

Former customers already know you. A structured win-back of lapsed customers is among the cheapest revenue sources — and almost always overlooked.

FAQ

How can I increase revenue quickly?

The fastest levers are usually the nearest ones: raise prices where you've been underpricing, sell more to existing customers, and close more of the deals already in your pipeline. These require no new lead generation and little extra cost — which is why they beat 'find more customers' on speed almost every time.

Is raising prices really the fastest way?

Often, yes. A price increase flows almost directly to revenue and profit, needs no new customers, and takes effect immediately. The barrier is rarely the market — it's the owner's fear of losing customers. In practice, a well-communicated, moderate increase loses few customers and raises revenue and margin at once.

How do I sell more to existing customers?

Start by understanding what else they need that you could provide — adjacent products, higher service tiers, more frequent purchases. Existing customers already trust you, so the sales cost is minimal. Many SMEs focus entirely on new customers and ignore the warm revenue sitting in their current base.

More leads or better closing — which first?

Usually better closing. Generating more leads is expensive; closing more of your existing leads is nearly free. If you're losing deals in follow-up or at the proposal stage, fixing that yields faster revenue than pouring more leads into a leaky funnel. Fix the leak before increasing the flow.
