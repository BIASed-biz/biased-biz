The local channel mix

Local visibility

has its own rules.

01

Google Business Profile & local SEO

The foundation. When neighbors search for your service, you must appear. This is the highest-leverage local channel — and the most commonly neglected.

02

Reviews from real local customers

Local trust is built on local reviews. A steady stream of genuine reviews from real customers signals to both neighbors and Google that you're the established local choice.

03

Local presence & partnerships

Being visibly part of the community — local partnerships, sponsorships, presence at the right local moments. For a local business, reputation travels by proximity and word of mouth.

04

What to leave out

National ad campaigns, broad social media, channels aimed at people outside your area — for a purely local business these often waste budget. Discipline about what not to do is half of local marketing.

FAQ

How do I make my local business better known?

Start with the foundation: a complete Google Business Profile and local SEO so you appear when neighbors search. Build a steady flow of genuine reviews from real customers. Be visibly part of the community. And deliberately skip the broad national channels that don't reach your local audience. Local fame is built on proximity, consistency and trust — not on big campaigns.

What's the most important channel for a local business?

For almost all local businesses, the Google Business Profile combined with local SEO is the single most important channel. It's where local buying decisions with immediate intent begin, it's free, and most competitors under-use it. A complete, active profile with genuine reviews often outweighs everything else for local discovery.

Should a local business be on social media?

Only if its customers are there and the effort returns more than the alternatives. For many local businesses — trades, B2B services, professional practices — the same effort in local SEO and reviews returns far more than social media. For visual, consumer-facing local businesses, social can work. The test is your specific customer, not the trend.

How long does it take to become known locally?

Local visibility builds steadily over months, not overnight. The Google Business Profile and local SEO can start showing effect within weeks; a strong local reputation with a steady review flow and community presence builds over a year. Consistency beats intensity — a little every month outperforms a big push that stops.
