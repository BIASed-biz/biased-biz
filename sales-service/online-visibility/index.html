FAQ

What is local SEO and why does it matter?

Local SEO is optimizing to appear when people search for your service in your area — the map results and 'near me' searches. It matters because most local buying decisions start with a search, and customers rarely look past the first few results. For a local SME, appearing there is often more valuable than ranking for broad national terms.

How important is a Google Business Profile?

For local businesses, it's among the most important free tools available. A complete, active profile — correct information, photos, posts, reviews — often determines whether you appear in local searches and the map. Many SMEs leave it half-filled or unclaimed, handing the visibility to competitors who took ten minutes to complete theirs.

How do I get more reviews?

By asking, systematically. Most satisfied customers would leave a review but never think to — so the businesses that simply ask, at the right moment, with an easy link, get far more. A structured, consistent approach to requesting reviews beats hoping they appear on their own. Never buy fake reviews — the risk far outweighs the benefit.

Do I need a website or is a Google profile enough?

For some very local businesses, a strong Google Business Profile can carry a lot. But for most, a website and a profile work together — the profile for local discovery, the website for depth, trust and answering questions. Which matters more depends on your business, and is worth deciding deliberately rather than by default.
