FAQ

What is B2B customer research?

B2B customer research is the systematic work of identifying and understanding the right target customers before selling to them — defining precisely who fits (industry, size, situation, decision-maker), finding where they are, and understanding what drives their buying. It turns scattered, hopeful outreach into focused effort aimed at prospects who can actually buy. It's the cheapest part of the sales process and the highest-leverage.

How do I find the right B2B customers?

Start by defining the ideal customer precisely, then use that profile to search deliberately — industry databases, trade associations, events, LinkedIn and digital footprints. A vague target ('any business that needs us') can't be found efficiently; a sharp one ('manufacturers of 50–200 employees with this specific problem') can be located and prioritized. Definition precedes discovery.

Why is market analysis worth it for an SME?

Because it stops you wasting scarce sales resources on the wrong segments. An SME can't afford to pursue every possible market. Market analysis reveals which segments offer the best ratio of opportunity to effort — the Pareto 20% worth focusing on — so limited sales energy goes where it returns most. It's focus applied before effort, not after.

Can I do B2B research myself?

Partly — the thinking (defining the ideal customer, prioritizing segments) is something every owner should engage with. The gathering (databases, systematic search, structured analysis) is where external support saves time and adds rigor.
