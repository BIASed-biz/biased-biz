The principle underneath everything

20% of the causes

create 80% of the results.

The Pareto principle is the core of the entire model. In every business, roughly 20% of customers create 80% of profit, 20% of process flaws cause 80% of friction. The art is not doing more — it's finding the few levers that actually move things. Every BI³AS³ed analysis starts with one question: which 20% matter most right now?
