FAQ

What's the difference to normal recruiting?

Normal recruiting works the active market — people who applied or are searching. Executive search for key positions reaches the passive market through targeted approach, and adds strategic context: understanding what the role really needs before searching. The difference shows in fit and in the cost of getting it wrong.

How much does filling a key position cost?

Executive search is typically success-based: 20–30% of the annual gross salary. For a key position, this is small against the cost of a mis-hire — 6–18 months' salary in direct cost, lost momentum and team disruption. BI³AS³ed discusses the structure individually.

How long does the search take?

Realistically 6–12 weeks from mandate to presenting suitable candidates, depending on role specificity and market availability. We communicate the realistic timeframe transparently from the start — no false promises.

Can this be combined with interim management?

Yes — and it's a common, sensible combination. An interim manager bridges the vacancy immediately while the executive search for the permanent hire runs in parallel. The position stays covered, and the permanent decision isn't rushed.
