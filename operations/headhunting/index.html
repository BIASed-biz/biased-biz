Why headhunting

Job ads find who's searching.

Headhunting finds who fits.

A job ad reaches people actively looking — often those who want to leave for a reason. The best candidates for your key position are employed and content enough not to search. But curious enough for the right conversation.

Headhunting means we go to those people actively — with a considered approach, a compelling picture of your company, and the tact these conversations require.

01

The active market is thin

Whoever answers a leadership ad competes with others — and often you don't get the best profiles. The passive market is larger and better qualified.

02

SMEs lose to corporations on job boards

On job boards an SME competes with DAX corporations for attention. Personal approach is where a well-run SME with real influence often has the better arguments.

03

Discretion is often mandatory

Replacing an existing position without the incumbent or team knowing prematurely rules out a public posting. Headhunting runs fully discreet.

04

A bad hire costs more than headhunting

A mis-hire in a key position costs 6–18 months' salary in direct cost, lost productivity and damage from wrong decisions.

FAQ

What does headhunting cost for an SME?

Headhunting is typically success-based: 20–30% of the annual gross salary of the filled position. For an €80,000 salary that's €16,000–24,000 — one-time, on successful placement. By comparison, a mis-hire costs 6–18 times the monthly salary. BI³AS³ed discusses the fee structure individually per mandate.

Is the direct approach of candidates legal?

Yes — with a clear boundary. A single direct approach of an employee at their workplace is generally permitted in Germany. Systematic, repeated poaching or using trade secrets is not. BI³AS³ed operates strictly within the legally sound framework — professional, discreet, respectful toward candidate and employer.

Which positions is headhunting worth for?

Whenever a position is strategically decisive — management, sales lead, production lead, CFO, successor, key developer — and a mis-hire would be more expensive than the search cost. For standard operational roles without strategic influence, classic postings are often more efficient.

How long does a search take?

Realistically 6–12 weeks from mandate to presenting suitable candidates. Factors: specificity of the role, market availability, geographic constraints, salary range. BI³AS³ed communicates transparently about market reality from the start.
