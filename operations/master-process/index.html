FAQ

How do I delegate without losing control?

Delegation without a system leads to chaos — which is what holds many owners back. The solution isn't less delegation, but more structure beforehand. Clear processes, measurable results and regular check-ins replace the owner's constant presence. Control then means: I see the result — not: I monitor every step.

Why do processes fail so often in SMEs?

The most common reasons: processes developed without the team (no buy-in), too complex for daily use (nobody reads them), not kept current (they age fast), not aligned to strategy, and the owner doesn't live them. A master process addresses all five at once.

Which tools are suitable?

Less than most think — and simpler than expected. The key isn't the tool but the structure behind it. BI³AS³ed implements the master process with a proven Master Tracker: a single file that works on any device, needs no training and is usable immediately.

How do I stop a new process ending in a drawer?

Three things: the team was involved from the start (their system, not a foreign one), it's simple enough to actually use, and there are regular review dates (quarterly, 30 minutes) where it's adjusted. What changes must change in the system.
