Definition

What is an interim manager —

and how is it different from a consultant?

An interim manager is an experienced executive who temporarily takes on an operational management role. Unlike a consultant who analyzes and recommends, an interim manager acts: they carry responsibility, make decisions and lead teams — with the same rights and duties as a permanent manager.

Billing is on a day-rate basis. No social contributions, no notice periods, no severance. A mandate typically runs 3 to 18 months — exactly as long as needed.

For SMEs this is especially valuable: they can bring board-level expertise in-house short-term that they couldn't or wouldn't afford permanently — solving exactly what needs solving now.

FAQ

What does an interim manager cost for an SME?

Day rates for interim managers in Germany range roughly from €800 to €1,800, depending on function, industry and mandate complexity. A rule of thumb: about 1% of the comparable annual gross salary per day. There are no social contributions, no paid leave, no sick pay. Compared to the opportunity cost of an unfilled leadership position, interim management is usually the more economical option.

How quickly is an interim manager available?

A key advantage over classic recruiting: availability. While a permanent leadership hire typically takes 3–6 months, a qualified interim manager can often start within 1–2 weeks. BI³AS³ed can present first qualified profiles within 48 hours.

When is an interim manager worth it?

Whenever leaving the position unfilled would cost more than the day rates: unexpected departure of a leader, upcoming succession, a transformation project without internal capacity, or a growth phase needing new structure. For standard operational roles, classic recruiting is often more efficient.

What's the difference to a consultant?

A consultant analyzes and recommends — implementation stays with the company. An interim manager takes operational responsibility, makes decisions and owns results. BI³AS³ed combines both: strategic advice and operational execution.

Can an interim manager be hired permanently afterwards?

Yes — and it's not an unusual outcome of a successful mandate. The company knows the person and trusts them. Many SMEs use interim management as an extended trial period at leadership level. A permanent hire is always possible — but doesn't have to be the goal.
